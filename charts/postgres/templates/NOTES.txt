PostgreSQL is deployed as a StatefulSet.

Connect from inside the cluster:

  host:     {{ include "postgres.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local
  port:     {{ .Values.service.port }}
  user:     {{ .Values.auth.username }}
  database: {{ .Values.auth.database }}
  password: kubectl get secret {{ include "postgres.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.POSTGRES_PASSWORD}' | base64 -d

Port-forward for local access:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "postgres.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} 5432:{{ .Values.service.port }}
